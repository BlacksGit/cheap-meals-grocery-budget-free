## 12 habits that cut the grocery bill

Plan meals first. Shop with a list. Check the pantry before you write it. Buy store brands. Buy frozen vegetables. Buy rice, beans and oats in bulk. Compare unit prices. Cook double and freeze half. Eat leftovers within 3 days. Eat before shopping. Limit pre-cut and pre-made foods. Track what you throw away.
